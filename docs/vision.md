# Vision

## Purpose

Quantum Economics exists to make **fair, transparent economic participation accessible to everyone** — regardless of geography, credit history, or institutional affiliation. The projects here build the infrastructure layer for community-first financial tools, grounded in open-source principles and rigorous engineering.

---

## Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Transparency** | All design decisions, AI usage, and tradeoffs are documented openly. |
| **Accessibility** | Tools are designed to work with minimal friction; no barriers to entry. |
| **Accountability** | Human review is required for all consequential decisions. AI assists; humans decide. |
| **Iterative Safety** | Ship small, verify often. No production-grade feature ships without a threat model review. |
| **Humanitarian Focus** | Every feature is evaluated against: "Does this reduce economic harm or expand access?" |

---

## Intended Impact

### Near-term (0–12 months)

- A working off-chain double-entry ledger (FreePay MVP) that community groups can deploy locally to track mutual-aid flows, group funds, or community currencies.
- Documented agent system capable of producing economic analysis reports without manual data processing.

### Medium-term (1–3 years)

- FreePay connected to real payment rails (Stripe, ACH, or stablecoin), enabling low-fee P2P transfers for under-served populations.
- Economics Lab producing reproducible research on informal economies, mutual aid networks, and alternative exchange systems.

### Long-term

- A reference ecosystem for developers building financial tools that put community benefit over profit extraction.
- An open dataset of economic experiments that can inform policy and tooling worldwide.

---

## Scope

**In scope:**

- Off-chain and (eventually) on-chain payment infrastructure
- AI agent tooling for economic analysis and workflow automation
- Research experiments, reproducible notebooks, evaluation frameworks
- Documentation, design artifacts, and architecture guidance

**Out of scope (for now):**

- Consumer-facing mobile apps (near-term)
- Regulated financial services (requires legal framework not yet in place)
- Real-money movement without full compliance review

---

## North Star Metric

> "Does this reduce the cost or complexity of economic participation for a person who currently has no good options?"

Every PR, feature, or experiment should have a plausible path to a "yes" answer to that question.
